What the terms mean

Basic PayAnnual salary before locality, premiums and differentials. Receives the full special 2.8% adjustment; used for retention, CIP and certain June lump sums.
Base PayAnnual salary with payable locality, excluding premiums and differentials. Statutory cap can prevent a matching increase.
Band maximumContractual ceiling relevant to regular band-based actions such as June adjustments. Does not prevent this special 2.8% adjustment.
Statutory cap2026 Base Pay ceiling of $228,000. Limits the salary-with-locality calculation; does not by itself cap total gross earnings including premiums.
Nominal vs effective localityNominal locality is the area's published rate; effective locality dollars may be reduced when Base Pay reaches the statutory cap.
FLSA regular rateSpecial overtime rate calculation that may incorporate other compensation, including CIP. It is not necessarily identical to your Base Pay hourly rate.
Scope and sources

This independent informational calculator uses the NATCA National Executive Board email, “2026 2.8% Pay Parity FAQ,” Oct. 30, 2026; the NATCA/FAA Slate Book (Article 108 and cited premium articles); 123ATC’s 313-facility directory and 2026 CPC/developmental Basic Pay bands; and OPM’s 2026 locality percentages. Facility locality rates were matched to the directory’s salary information where possible or geographically mapped to OPM localities, and may need checking against the linked facility page. Some optional CIP percentages were inferred from 123ATC entry-level total-pay differences: these are estimates, not a confirmation of individual CIP eligibility. Other special differentials (e.g. nonforeign COLA, post, affordability) are not included in the CIP estimate or the raise comparison. Sources: NATCA raise announcement, 2026 NY locality, Slate Book reference (independent). The Oct. 30 FAQ was supplied by the user; it is not automatically fetched by this page.

Important: These projections do not include actual historical pay-period changes, special rounding, shifts, absences, varying hours, leave, other premium entitlements, tax withholding, retirement contributions, bonuses not based on Basic Pay, or payroll reconciliation. They are not a substitute for official FAA/NATCA calculations. NATCA said its official backpay tool was planned for release separately. Facility pay bands, locality percentages, and CIP snapshots reflect 2026 and must be updated for future years.

Privacy: This single HTML file runs entirely in your browser; no salary data is uploaded, transmitted or saved to a server. Export and print operate locally.
